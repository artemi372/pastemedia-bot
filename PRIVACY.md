# Privacy Policy

This policy covers the official bot run by [@artemi372](https://t.me/artemi372) (the "author"), based on the open-source code at <https://github.com/artemi372/pastemedia-bot>. It's a free, non-commercial hobby project. For the rules of using the bot, see the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md).

## What the bot collects and why

| Data | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your Telegram user ID and chosen language, only if you change the language | to answer you in that language | until you ask to delete it |
| Group IDs and group settings (language, notices, `/cleanup` mode) | to work in the group the way its admins set it up | until the bot leaves or is removed from the group |
| IDs of recent bot messages in groups, with the video they contain | to avoid posting the same video twice | up to a week |
| Copies of downloaded videos and photos with their source link, in a private storage channel | so the same video isn't downloaded again | until deleted on request or by the author |
| Server logs: chat IDs, links sent to the bot, errors | to find and fix problems | for a limited time |

The bot **doesn't** collect your phone number, contacts or location, and doesn't keep the text of your messages. In groups, it reads messages only to find supported links. Everything else is ignored and not stored.

When `/cleanup` is on in a group, the text you wrote next to a link is copied under the posted video. That's part of the group's chat history, not a separate record kept by the bot.

## Who can see it

Only the author. Data is not sold, shared or used for ads or analytics. It may be disclosed only if required by law or by Telegram's rules.

The data is stored on a rented server in the EU and in Telegram (the storage channel). Telegram's own [Privacy Policy](https://telegram.org/privacy) applies to everything you send through Telegram.

## Your rights

You can ask to see the data about you, or to delete it, or to remove a stored copy of a video. Write to [@artemi372](https://t.me/artemi372). Requests are handled as soon as possible.

You can also simply stop using the bot or remove it from your group at any time.

## Changes

This policy can change. The current version is always in this file.
