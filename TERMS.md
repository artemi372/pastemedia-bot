- A free, non-commercial hobby project made by one person ([@artemi372](https://t.me/artemi372)), not a company or a service with support staff.
- It downloads **publicly available** videos and photos from TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram by the link you send, and posts them to the chat.
- It is **not affiliated** with TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Meta, Google or Telegram.

## 2. Your responsibility

You, not the bot or its author, are responsible for every link you send and every chat you send it to. In particular:

- **Only share content you're allowed to share.** A video being public on one platform doesn't give you the right to repost it anywhere. Respect the creators.
- **No illegal or harmful content**: no sexual content involving minors, no non-consensual intimate images, no terrorism or violent extremism, no doxxing, no harassment. Such content will be removed and reported to Telegram.
- **Don't use the bot to hide who you are.** In groups where messages are cleaned up, the text you write next to a link may be reposted by the bot. You're still the author of it.
- **Don't spam or overload the bot**: no floods of links, no scripts, no attempts to break it.
- **Don't send private content**, such as videos from private accounts or content shared with you in confidence.

## 3. Group admins

If you add the bot to a group, you're responsible for how it's used there.

- With `/cleanup`, the bot **deletes the original message** with the link and reposts the media. Depending on the mode, the reposted message shows the link, the sender and the sender's comment. Choose the mode that fits your group's rules.
- The bot needs admin rights only to delete messages. Don't give it more than that.
- You can remove the bot from your group at any time.
